# App Description - Bulk SMS Sender

This document provides a comprehensive description of the **Bulk SMS Sender** app, suitable for the Google Play Store or other marketing materials.

---

## 📝 Short Description (80 characters)
Fast, secure bulk SMS sender using your own SIM plan. No third-party accounts.

---

## 📄 Long Description

**Send personalized SMS to multiple recipients effortlessly using your device's native SIM and carrier plan.**

Bulk SMS Sender is a powerful, lightweight utility designed for individuals and small businesses who need a reliable way to reach their contacts, customers, or community members without relying on expensive third-party SMS gateways.

Unlike other apps, Bulk SMS Sender works directly with your phone's built-in SMS manager. This means your messages are sent using your existing mobile plan, keeping costs transparent and your data private.

### 🚀 Key Features:

*   **Native SIM Integration**: Use your own carrier plan and SIM card directly.
*   **Dual SIM Support**: Easily choose between active SIM cards on supported devices to manage your carrier limits and costs.
*   **Flexible Recipient Import**:
    *   Manual entry (one per line, comma, or semicolon).
    *   Import directly from your device's Contacts.
    *   Bulk import from CSV files for large lists.
*   **Intelligent Parsing**: Automatically cleans formatted phone numbers and handles "Name, Number" pairs.
*   **Automatic Deduplication**: Never spam the same person twice; the app automatically filters duplicate numbers.
*   **Personalization**: Include recipient names in your messages for a more professional and personal touch.
*   **Paced Sending**: Adjustable delay between messages (300ms to 5000ms) to ensure carrier compliance and prevent throttling.
*   **Real-time Tracking**: Monitor Sent, Delivered, and Failed counts with a live progress bar.
*   **Transparent Status**: View a detailed breakdown of failed numbers at the end of every batch.
*   **Privacy First**: No third-party servers, no cloud storage, and no analytics. Your data stays on your device.

### 🛠️ Technical Details & Requirements:
*   **Platform**: Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and above.
*   **Permissions**:
    *   `SEND_SMS`: Required to deliver your messages.
    *   `READ_PHONE_STATE`: Required for Dual SIM detection.
    *   `READ_CONTACTS`: (Optional) For importing from your address book.
*   **Battery Optimized**: Pinned "Send" and "Cancel" buttons ensure the app is easy to use even when the keyboard is open.

### 💡 Why Choose Bulk SMS Sender?
*   **No Subscription**: Pay only what your carrier charges for SMS.
*   **Fast & Reliable**: Written in native Kotlin for maximum performance and stability.
*   **Simple UI**: Clean Material 3 design makes bulk messaging intuitive for everyone.
