# Privacy Policy for Bulk SMS Sender

**Last Updated**: July 25, 2026

**Introsoft** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, **Bulk SMS Sender**, handles information when you use the app.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Bulk SMS Sender** is designed as a local utility tool. We prioritize your privacy by ensuring that your data remains on your device.

### A. Personal Data
We **do not collect, store, or transmit** any personal data to external servers. All data you input or import into the app stays strictly on your mobile device.

### B. Permissions Used
To provide its core functionality, the app requires the following permissions:

*   **SEND_SMS**: Required to send the text messages you compose to your specified recipients using your mobile carrier plan.
*   **READ_PHONE_STATE**: Required to detect active SIM cards on your device, allowing you to choose which SIM to use for sending messages (Dual SIM support).
*   **READ_CONTACTS**: (Optional) Used only if you choose to import recipients from your device's address book.
*   **Storage Access**: Used only to allow you to select and import CSV files containing recipient information.

### C. Data Processing
*   **Recipients & Messages**: The list of phone numbers and the messages you write are processed in real-time to execute the sending task. This data is not saved by us and is lost when the app is cleared from memory, unless you have entered it into the input fields.
*   **No Third-Party Tracking**: We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK).

---

## 2. Data Security

Since the app does not transmit data over the internet (it does not even require the `INTERNET` permission), your data is as secure as your physical device. We recommend using standard Android security features (like screen locks) to protect the information on your phone.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The app interacts with your mobile carrier's SMS infrastructure to deliver messages. When a message is sent, it is subject to your carrier's privacy policy and terms of service. We do not have control over how your carrier handles the metadata of the messages sent.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
