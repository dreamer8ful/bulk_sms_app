## Full App Description (4000 character limit)

```
**Bulk SMS Sender** - Send SMS messages to hundreds of contacts at once!

## What It Does
Send one message to a list of phone numbers using your phone's own SIM and carrier plan via Android's native SmsManager API. No third-party services. No subscriptions. Just your phone's carrier plan.

## Key Features
✓ Bulk SMS sending - Send to hundreds of contacts in one batch
✓ Multiple input methods:
  - Import phone numbers manually (one per line, comma, or semicolon separated)
  - Import from device contacts (with names)
  - Load from CSV file
  - Automatic duplicate removal

✓ Customizable messaging:
  - Full message editor
  - Preview before sending
  - Automatic multipart SMS handling (long messages split automatically)
  - Personalized messages with recipient names

✓ Advanced controls:
  - Adjustable delay between sends (1-5000ms)
  - Real-time progress tracking
  - Sent/Failed/Delivered counts
  - Failed number logging for retry

✓ Smart delivery tracking:
  - Real-time sent status
  - Delivery confirmations (if carrier supports)
  - Failed SMS logging with error details
  - Toast notifications for each message

## How to Use
1. Type your message in the message box
2. Enter recipient phone numbers (one per line, or import from contacts/CSV)
3. Adjust delay if needed (default: 1.5 seconds)
4. Tap "Send to all numbers"
5. Grant SMS permission when prompted
6. Watch progress in real-time with status updates

## Perfect For
• Broadcasting announcements to teams or groups
• Quick customer notifications
• Event updates and reminders
• Emergency alerts
• Community communications
• Small business notifications

## Requirements
• Android 5.0+ (API 21)
• Active mobile carrier plan
• SIM card with SMS capability
• Permissions: Send SMS, Read Phone State, Read Contacts (optional)

## Important Notes
• Uses YOUR phone's carrier plan and SIM - all costs are covered by your existing plan
• NOT a third-party SMS service - all messages sent directly from your device
• Keep app in foreground while sending (backgrounding may stop the batch)
• Carrier fair-use limits may apply
• No internet connection required (uses device SMS capabilities)

## Privacy
• No data collection or analytics
• No internet permissions
• All processing happens on your device
• No servers or external services

## Permissions Explained
• SEND_SMS: Core feature to send bulk messages
• READ_PHONE_STATE: Access to phone information for SMS sending
• READ_CONTACTS: Optional - to import contacts as recipients

## Troubleshooting
• "Failed to send": Check phone number format, carrier service, or delay
• "Permission denied": Grant SMS permissions when prompted
• "No contacts found": Enable contact permissions or enter numbers manually

## Tips for Best Results
• Use international format for numbers: +1 followed by area code and number
• Set delay higher if messages are failing (1-2 seconds minimum)
• Send in batches of <500 numbers for best performance
• Keep device powered on during sending
• Test with small group first

## Support
For issues or feature requests, contact the developer through Google Play Store.
```
